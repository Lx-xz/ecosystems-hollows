# Cobblemon Ecosystems: Hollows, Chasms and Cavities

**Alex's Caves compat for Cobblemon Ecosystems · Compat do Cobblemon Ecosystems com o Alex's Caves**

<details>
<summary><b>🇺🇸 English</b></summary>

An add-on for [Cobblemon Ecosystems](https://github.com/Lx-xz/living-fauna-flora) and Raguto's
**Alex's Caves (Unofficial Port)**. The animals and fantasy creatures of the caves give way to Pokémon, some of them
with **regional forms** of their own, like Alolan and Galarian forms. Hostile mobs and cave inhabitants stay.

### ✅ Candy Cavity
- **Candyan Stufful** (Fairy/Normal) and **Candyan Bewear** (Fairy/Fighting): translucent jelly bears in six colors,
  replacing the Gummy Bear
- **Candyan Orthworm** (Fairy/Ground): a striped bubblegum worm with 10 tail segments, replacing the Gum Worm

### 🗺️ Planned
- **Primordial Caves**: living fossil Pokémon instead of the dinosaurs
- **Toxic Caves**: a glowing green Grimer and other Poison Pokémon
- **Abyssal Chasm**: Chinchou, Lanturn, Pyukumuku, Finneon and Relicanth
- **Forlorn Hollows**: Venomoth, Dustox, Zubat, Noibat, Diglett and Drilbur
- **Magnetic Caves**: Magnemite, Nosepass, Klink and Beldum
- **Candy Cavity**: Candyan Ponyta and Rapidash, Swirlix, Milcery and Alcremie
- Deep Ones, Underzealots and Licowitches with their own Pokémon

### Requirements
- Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge
- Cobblemon 1.8+ and Kotlin for Forge
- Cobblemon Ecosystems 1.0+
- Alex's Caves (Unofficial Port) 2.0+ and Citadel (Unofficial Port)

## Development · Desenvolvimento

```bash
./gradlew build            # build/libs/cobblemon-ecosystems-hollows-<version>.jar
./gradlew runClient        # test client (needs ../base built first: Cobblemon Ecosystems)
```

Textures and the Candyan Orthworm model are generated by the scripts in [`tools/`](tools/) (Python + Pillow) from
Cobblemon's own assets. The full plan is in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) (Portuguese).
